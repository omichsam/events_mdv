    Help and policies

    How booking works, paying with card or mobile money, refunds, and listing an event.

    Booking

    Each event page has a Tickets box that shows how that event sells:

    • Book here. Choose tickets, add names, and pay by card or mobile money. Seats are held while you pay. Your QR tickets are in My tickets and your email.
    • Organizer’s site. The button opens the organizer’s ticket site. They handle payment and refunds.
    • Register. Free events ask for your name and email. Invitation-only events also need the code on your invitation.

    Paying with mobile money

    Choose mobile money at checkout and enter your phone number. Approve the payment on your phone (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, or MTN MoMo, depending on the country). Prices are in the event’s local currency; an approximate US dollar amount is shown for visitors.

    Refunds and changes

    • Full refund up to 7 days before the event, minus the booking fee.
    • Inside 7 days, tickets can be transferred to another name but not refunded.
    • If an event is cancelled or moved, everyone is refunded in full.
    • Request a refund or a name change from My tickets. Mobile money refunds arrive within 3 working days; card refunds take 5–10.

    At the door

    Show the QR code on your phone or on paper. Each code works once. For online events, the joining link is in your ticket email.

    For organizers

    We can list your event and link to your ticket site, or sell tickets for you with ticket types, promo codes, a waitlist, guest lists, and door check-in. Payouts are made after the event, less the booking fee.

    Write to hello@mdv.example with the dates, venue, and how you want to sell. New listings are reviewed before they go live.

    Terms

    A ticket is a licence to attend one event, for the named holder. The organizer runs the event and is responsible for its content and safety. Tickets may not be resold above face value.

    Privacy

    We keep the name, email, and phone you give at checkout to send tickets and updates, and share the guest list with that event’s organizer. Card and mobile money details are handled by the payment provider. We follow the data protection laws of the countries we operate in. Write to hello@mdv.example to see or delete your data.

    Questions

    Which payment methods work?

    Visa and Mastercard, and mobile money: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and MTN MoMo where available.

    Which currency will I pay in?

    The event’s local currency, for example KES in Nairobi or UGX in Kampala. The US dollar figure is approximate.

    Is there a booking fee?

    A small percentage per order on tickets sold here, shown before you pay.
